# Shortlist — method, exclusions, and what this data is not

Search run 15 September 2026. **298 postings pulled from LinkedIn, 41 kept.**

---

## How the match score works

| Component | Max | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Title fit** | 30 | How closely the posted title maps to one of your 20 target titles. |
| **Level fit** | 30 | Whether you can actually apply *today*. Intern and explicit early-career postings score high; "Senior", "Lead" and graded bank titles score low regardless of how well the work matches. |
| **Skill overlap** | 25 | Proportion of the role's stated stack you already evidence. Gaps you don't have (Power BI, SAS, C++, Spark) pull this down. |
| **Employer signal** | 15 | Whether the employer is real, reputable and worth your application time. |
| **Match score** | 100 | Sum of the four. A ranking device, not a probability of getting hired. |

**Verdict bands:** `Apply this week` 85–100 · `Strong – apply` 78–84 · `Worth a shot` 70–77 · `Stretch` below 70

| Excluded | What it looked like | Why |

| **Devryxa · Dexter's Tech · Vortenza Systems** | ~12 near-identical "Data Science Intern", "ML Intern", "Business Analytics Intern" posts, all dated the same day, no company footprint | Lead-generation listings. Applying hands over your contact details and nothing else. |
| **Crossing Infotech · MediNex Workforce** | Keyword-stuffed titles ("Entry Level \| Fresher \| Python, Pandas, SQL \| Remote"), posted in duplicate under both names | The same posting under two staffing shells — a resume-harvesting pattern. |
| **Unified Mentor · internmo · UM IT Solutions · SKOOLLAGE** | "Internships" that are paid training programmes | You pay them. Courses sold as jobs. |
| **Nexal IIT · Zetheta Algorithms · F6IT · TalentXM** | Unverifiable entities posting senior titles at intern volume | No verifiable company behind the listing. |
| **VRINDA INTERNATIONAL · SourcingXPress · ASTIKA · Hire Feed · all things talent** | Generic "Data Scientist" reposts across multiple cities | Recruiter reposts of other firms' reqs. Apply to the actual employer. |
| **Bain & Company — Intern Analyst, Batch of 2027** | Four postings, each named for a specific campus (VNIT Nagpur, PEC Chandigarh) | Campus-exclusive. You are not eligible from BIT Mesra. |
| **NVIDIA — PhD Intern, AI ML in Wireless** | Explicitly PhD-only | Hard eligibility gate. |
| **~110 Senior / Principal / Staff / Lead / VP / Manager Data Scientist roles** | Walmart, Microsoft, Atlassian, Citi, Fractal, NielsenIQ, Amgen, Takeda, Mastercard and others | 6–12+ years required. **LinkedIn's "entry level" filter returned these anyway** — it filters on the poster's tag, not the actual requirement. |
| **EXL — Senior Executive / Assistant Manager / Lead Assistant Manager** | Eight postings | Mid-career grades despite analyst-sounding titles. |
| **WorldQuant · Millennium · Trexquant (Experienced Hires) · Goldman Associate · Morgan Stanley Manager** | Quant roles at experienced-hire grade | Right domain, wrong level. Revisit after two years or a strong internship. |

## Honest caveats — read these before trusting the sheet

**Scores are judgement, not measurement.** They were read off the posting title, company, level and your CV — **not** the full job description text. Re-check against the actual JD before you apply.

**Indeed is not in this data.** Indeed's bot protection refused the session after two attempts. Its listings largely duplicate LinkedIn's, so the loss is small, but it is a real gap.

**Coverage is six searches, not twenty.** LinkedIn's guest view cut the session off after six queries. Families covered: *data science intern · machine learning intern · data analyst intern · quantitative analyst · decision scientist · data scientist*. Not separately searched: data engineer, business analyst, product analyst, credit risk, BI analyst, statistical programmer, operations research. Those titles are under-represented here — run them yourself when you want more volume.

**Postings expire.** Everything here was live on 15 Sep 2026. Anything posted before 1 September may already be closed — check before writing a tailored version.

**One posting worth noting that didn't make the list:** **Amgen — Senior Data Scientist, Hyderabad.** Too senior for you by years, so it is excluded. But you named your original resume for Amgen, so it is worth knowing they are actively hiring in Hyderabad. Set a LinkedIn alert on Amgen India and use the `PHA` CV the moment an associate or graduate seat appears.
