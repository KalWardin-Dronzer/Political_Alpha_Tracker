## WHY THIS IS URGENT, NOT COSMETIC

LinkedIn is not a second résumé. It is a **searchable index**, and recruiters work it with LinkedIn Recruiter — the same boolean strings already in your keyword map. An empty profile isn't a profile that looks bad. It's a profile that **does not appear in results at all**. You are invisible to every inbound search, permanently, until this is filled.

It also breaks the referral plan. Every alumni message you send lands on someone whose first move is to click your profile. An empty one gets no reply, and a referrer who does look will quietly decide not to vouch.

**Search weighting is uneven.** LinkedIn Recruiter leans hardest on **Headline**, **current position title**, **Skills**, and **About** — not on the whole profile equally. Spend your effort there in that order.

- **Job types:** Internship **and** Full-time
- **Start date:** immediately available for internships
- **Locations:** Bengaluru · Hyderabad · Gurugram · Mumbai · Pune · Chennai · Remote
  > Set these here rather than faking your profile location. Recruiters filter on these preferences, and your actual location stays honest.

**Custom URL** → `linkedin.com/in/vivek-kumar-qeds` or similar. The default string of digits looks unfinished on a CV.

**Photo** → plain background, face filling most of the frame, neutral expression. Profiles with a photo get many times more views; without one, a referrer has no reason to trust the account is real.

**Banner** → anything clean and non-default. A plain gradient beats the grey placeholder.

## 11. AFTER IT'S BUILT

Recruiters check whether an account is alive. A profile with zero activity reads as abandoned.

- **Connections:** get past 100 quickly — BIT Mesra alumni, classmates, seniors already placed. Below ~50, LinkedIn deprioritizes you in search and your profile looks dormant.
- **Activity:** two or three substantive comments a week on posts from people in your target companies. Not "Great post!" — an actual observation. This is also the softest possible first touch before you send a referral ask.
- **One post:** when you push the Political Alpha Tracker, write a short post about one specific thing you learned building it. Not a job-hunting appeal. A technical note gets shared; a "kindly refer me" post gets scrolled past.
