**The Bottleneck:**
The graph requires mapping directors to these companies to find political connections. Our `MCAResolver` scrapes the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Zaubacorp to get this director data, but it strictly requires a **CIN (Corporate Identification Number)** to perform the lookup. 

Previously, the pipeline had a hardcoded list of `KNOWN_CINS` for about 10 government companies. Because our new expanded list of private companies don't have their CINs hardcoded, the pipeline skips them.
